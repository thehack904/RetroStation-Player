Pi Zero W Streaming Notice

The original Raspberry Pi Zero W has limited processing capacity. Incoming streams above SD resolution, high-bitrate streams, or formats that cannot use hardware decoding may stutter, buffer, lose synchronization, or fail to play smoothly—especially over composite output.

For the most reliable playback, use a 720×480 or lower source stream. RetroStation Player will start channels immediately and will not inspect each stream before playback.
